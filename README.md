# Netbalance Revenue Recovery Agent

An autopilot for finance teams that investigates cash issues and works until the outcome is verified.

## Market Problem

Most finance AI identifies problems but does not finish the work. Recovering cash requires working across internal systems, external data, documents, and customer portals until the money is recovered.

## Use Case

The CFO of a data center company asks:

> Why is cash down this month?

## How It Works

1. Netbalance finds that collections are down $620K.
2. It identifies a $42K deduction taken by Atlas Cloud because of a reported power outage.
3. Tavily confirms that a local grid outage occurred.
4. PRISM determines that the evidence is incomplete and identifies the missing power failover report.
5. Netbalance retrieves the report, confirms that backup power prevented an SLA breach, and concludes that the deduction is invalid.
6. The agent submits the claim, monitors repayment, and reconciles the recovered cash.

## Tools

- **ElevenLabs** provides the CFO voice interface.
- **Tavily** verifies external information.
- **PRISM** checks the evidence and verifies the outcome.
- **Netbalance** manages the financial investigation and execution.

## Business Value

Netbalance reduces manual investigation, accelerates cash recovery, and enables safer AI-driven finance operations.
